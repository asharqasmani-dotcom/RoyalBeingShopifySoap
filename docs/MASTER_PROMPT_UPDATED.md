# 12. Content and Claims Safety

- Preserve the client’s source copy, but flag strong therapeutic statements for owner/legal review before publishing, especially claims involving acne, eczema, psoriasis, antibacterial effects, stress, sleep, headaches, inflammation, healing, detoxification, age reversal, or treatment.

- Do not write new medical claims. Prefer cosmetic phrasing such as “helps skin feel,” “supports the appearance of,” “gently cleanses,” and “leaves skin feeling.”

- Do not fabricate certifications, dermatologist approval, clinical testing, customer counts, testimonials, awards, sustainability measurements, or manufacturing claims.

- Add an appropriate cosmetic disclaimer and allergy/patch-test guidance only after owner approval.

Royal being’s Visual language should communicate our specific brand ethos before the  
customer even reads the label. We can lean into organic textures, luxurious minimalism, or bold  
maximalism, a cohesive visual system is key to capturing the attention of our customers.  
Examples of powerful visual languages used in the soap industry include:  
• Luxe Apothecary & Minimalist: Features stark, clean lines, neutral colors (like cream, beige,  
or matte black), and understated serif or sans-serif typography. Popularized by brands like  
Aesop, this style avoids loud illustrations and instead lets the structural design and material  
(like amber glass bottles or simple embossed paper) signal premium quality.  
• Organic & Earthy: Appeals to eco-conscious consumers by using kraft paper wraps, twine,  
wax seals, and botanical, hand-drawn illustrations. The color palette relies on earthy browns,  
muted greens, and soft pastels to highlight natural ingredients.  
• Window Cut-Outs & Transparency: Allows the customer to see the soap's natural swirl,  
color, or embedded botanicals. You can pair these see-through or die-cut windows with bold,  
to build trust and highlight the handmade quality of your product.  
• Bold "Human Chaos": Serves as a counter-movement to AI-generated perfection. By using  
misregistered colors, graffiti, hand-drawn typography, and Chemicolor, brands create a  
vibrant, highly tactile visual identity that is impossible to ignore on crowded shelves or digital  
feeds.  
• Geometric & Modern: Utilizes clean lines, circles, squares, and triangles paired with  
contrasting, vibrant color palettes. This cutting-edge aesthetic is highly effective for  
contemporary brands looking to attract shoppers with sleek, sophisticated tastes.  
To effectively implement these concepts, carefully consider your typography, color psychology,  
and Label Design Tricks to ensure consistency across our brand and website touch points.
